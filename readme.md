## About

Hi, I'm Zetsukae. I made Underfang: Prologue. It's a short psychological
horror experience where you play as a child who faints while playing with
his cousin, and has to navigate surreal dreamscapes to find a way back to
reality.

## Gameplay

- Sprint & Survive: Run to escape nightmares, but manage your stamina bar
  carefully.
- Dream Exploration: Venture through multiple dreams that blur the line
  between reality and madness.
- Short & Reflective: A brief adventure focused on atmosphere and
  introspection.
- This prologue offers no help in finding one’s way through the story. You
  will need some patience.

## Features

- Dreamcore and glitchcore aesthetic
- Loop-based dream sequences
- Partial controller support
- English and French language support
- Mods support

## System Requirements (Minimum)

- OS: Windows 10
- Processor: Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3
- Memory: 8 GB RAM
- Graphics: Intel HD Graphics 620 or equivalent
- DirectX: Version 11
- Storage: 1 GB available space
